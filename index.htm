Measuring strip lengths

Every strip glows warm white, and its last LED is red.

Raise the number until the red LED disappears — that means it has run past the physical end of the strip. Then step back down by one until it lights again. That number is the strip's length.

While measuring, the strips are temporarily stretched to 300 LEDs. Cancel restores the previous settings.
